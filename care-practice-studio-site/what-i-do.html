What I do

Planning first, then an AI-assisted build.

Care leads, and the tool follows — not the other way. The hope is for one person who will learn the problem, plan it, then build it, and a clear look at how the work actually goes matters before anyone says yes.

  1. 01
    Understand

    We listen.

    What’s your real problem, and what do you hope to achieve?

  2. 02
    Plan

    We plan.

    A clear, actionable roadmap for your product.

  3. 03
    Build

    We build.

    We coordinate between talented engineers, designers, and frontier AI models to bring your solution to life.

  4. 04
    Check in

    We check in.

    After launch, we review how you’re using it — and help you decide what to do next.

    Included: a 3-hour post-launch check-in

    We look at how you’re actually using the product, confirm you’re happy with it, and review real usage data together. Then we decide next steps together — a fix, a new feature, or a separate project.
