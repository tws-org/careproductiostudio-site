The tools close at hand were not shaped for small practices

Care Practice Studio works alongside solo practitioners, small nonprofits, and community clinics. Days in this work are already full. A modest, real-world budget can still support a clear mission.

Big hospital systems already have vendors built for them. Directory sites mostly want another name on a list. Generic web shops tend to hand everyone the same site.

That still leaves room for behavioral health, mental health, and a wellness practice that stays small on purpose.

Those providers do not need a lead list. What fits is a product shaped around how people are actually helped.

One client. One custom product.

Care Practice Studio is a solo consultancy, and that focus is a gift. Peter plans and builds custom digital products with real care for behavioral and mental health providers, wellness practices, solo practitioners, small nonprofits, and community clinics.

Each job is one-on-one. One client at a time, so the work gets his full attention — closer and more personal than a large hospital-affiliated system, and nothing like a directory or lead-gen site.

Straight answers before the work begins

A practice can feel too small for a product studio.

Small is exactly who this work is for. Solo practitioners, wellness practices, small nonprofits, and community clinics in behavioral and mental health are the heart of the studio — and a wonderful place to begin.

Is Care Practice Studio a web agency?

No — and that distinction is a strength. Care Practice Studio is technical product management. Peter plans and builds custom digital products with care, rather than running a generic web shop.

Does the studio serve hospital systems?

No. Large hospital-affiliated systems sit outside this focus, so care and attention stay with smaller, mission-driven teams.

Does the work place a practice on a directory?

No. Directory and lead-gen sites are a different business. The aim is a product made for one team, not a public listing.

Does the studio already hold deep compliance credentials?

No. That experience is not claimed here. When a product needs a compliance review, the right reviewer belongs alongside the build — Peter will gladly work with that reviewer and will not pretend to fill that role.

When a practice fits this page

Care Practice Studio works with one client at a time, so a small practice receives real attention. The studio is glad to support mission-driven behavioral and mental health providers — wellness practices, solo practitioners, small nonprofits, and community clinics — whose care is rooted in the community rather than in large hospital-affiliated systems or in directory and lead-gen sites.
